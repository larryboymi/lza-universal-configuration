
== Service Quotas

=== Overview

Certain AWS Service Quotas may need to be increased before deploying the LZA Universal Configuration. This section documents quotas that commonly require adjustment based on your deployment scope.

=== AWS Organizations Account Quota

AWS Organizations has a default maximum of 10 accounts per organization. Newly created organizations may start with a quota lower than 10. The LZA Universal Configuration creates at least 6 accounts during initial deployment (LogArchive, Audit, Network, Perimeter, SharedServices, and the pre-existing Management account). Additional workload accounts increase this total.

Before deploying for the first time, verify your account quota and request an increase if needed. The LZA pipeline will fail to create accounts once the quota is reached.

==== Requesting a Quota Increase

1. Sign in to the Management account
2. Open the link:https://console.aws.amazon.com/servicequotas/home/services/organizations/quotas[Service Quotas console for AWS Organizations]
3. Select **Default maximum number of accounts**
4. Choose **Request increase at account level**
5. Enter the desired value based on your planned account count

[NOTE]
====
An invitation sent to an existing account also counts against this quota. If you plan to invite existing accounts into the organization, include those in your total.
====

For the full list of AWS Organizations quotas, see link:https://docs.aws.amazon.com/organizations/latest/userguide/orgs_reference_limits.html[Quotas for AWS Organizations].
